# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project that helps school staff get an
administrative job done; there is nothing here worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about a technical trade-off,
  not about a person.
- Give feedback on the work, not the author. "This breaks the draft-save path
  when the month changes" is useful. "This is sloppy" is not.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. A rejected pull request is not
  a judgement of you, and the reason should be stated plainly.
- Remember that many contributors are not writing in their first language.
  Read for intent before reacting to phrasing.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks; discriminatory or demeaning comments
about anyone's background or identity; sexualised language or imagery;
publishing anyone's private information; and sustained disruption of
discussions.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else the
project is being discussed.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer privately at
**eyyup.hikmet@outlook.com** rather than escalating in a public thread.
Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this
document, and may block repeat offenders from the project. The intent is to
keep the project usable and pleasant, not to police tone.
